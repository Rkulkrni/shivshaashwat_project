About Shivshaashwat Foundation

Committed to humanity, service, and social responsibility

Who We Are

Shivshaswat Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving lives through education, healthcare, blood donation, and social welfare activities.

For the past 10 years, our foundation has worked tirelessly with volunteers, donors, and partner organizations to support underprivileged communities.

We believe in transparency, compassion, and sustainable development for a better future.

Our vision

To create a compassionate society where every individual has access to education, healthcare, and equal opportunities.

Our mission

To serve humanity through social initiatives, blood donation awareness, educational support, and community welfare programs.
